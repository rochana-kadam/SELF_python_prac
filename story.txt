A thirsty crow flew across a dry field in search of water.
After a long search, it finally spotted a pitcher with a little water at the bottom.
The crow tried to drink, but its beak could not reach the water level.
It gathered small pebbles and dropped them into the pitcher one by one until the water rose to the top.
The crow happily drank the water and flew away, proving that perseverance and problem-solving find a way.
